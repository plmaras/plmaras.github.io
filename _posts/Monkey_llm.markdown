We all know about [infinite monkey theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_monkey_theorem) where if we have infinte monkeys each with infinte type writers and have infinite time where each monkey is randomly pressing the typewriter. In theory one monkey could have written the script of titanic, other could have written a better ending to Game thrones, Some monkeys might as well written proofs to different open problems (there may exsits some proofs which are not provable in the same systems (Godel's Theorem) i rember this vaguely may be i am wrong). I think llms are similar to them in some sense i could be wrong.

 The main thing that made llms click are attention  module + scalling laws . While caluclating attention we look back at every token in past we don't maintain a running memory and also save all the previous tokens in form KV-Cache. While humans don't need to think back that much and have important things in their main memory. Now, comming back to the title i might have exagerrated it a bit, In infinite Monkeys the Monkeys are hopeless and do things randomly, instead in LLMs we are using Data to train and align it so that it performs our task and also instead of making them type randomly we are giving a probablilty distribution over set of tokens to predict next tokens. What i meant was by training we are reducing the large out space given by random monkeys to making it into small useful out space with proper priors on words by training more and more sophisticated models we are searching and improving this out space more effectively,this is analogous to taking all these monkeys and letting them keep some parts of knowledge from books in their hand and making them write and rewarding them based on response. Now, using these trained monkeys we select the  best monkey based on our prior and make them write, but there is still a doubt regarding how well do they understand and innovate in there expertise. 
 
 So my theory is that they don't have real consciousness, they may have some understanding of the world. **I am also not saying they are useless or something they are very usefull and are revolutionzing many indsutry and even pushing frontier in science too**. Though no human fully understands these Lean - Formalized Proofs and many mathematicians also want to use AI but that usage must help them progress and provide exposition but don't what these unexplainable lean verified solutions.
